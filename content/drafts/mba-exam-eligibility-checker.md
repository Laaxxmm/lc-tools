### 5. My marks card shows CGPA and no percentage. Which number do I enter?

The tool page already answers this:

> Use your university’s own conversion, because that is the rule the exam body applies. The IIMs say so in the CAT notification: they take the university’s stated conversion, and where the university confirms it has none, they divide your CGPA by the maximum possible CGPA and multiply by 100. VTU is the one to watch in Karnataka, since it subtracts 0.75 before multiplying by 10, and that gap decides eligibility for anybody sitting near 50%.

### 7. Can a B.Com or B.A. graduate write NIMCET?

The tool page already answers this:

> No. NIMCET is the MCA test for the NITs, and it names the degrees it accepts: a three-year B.Sc, B.Sc (Hons), BCA or BIT, or a B.E./B.Tech. You also need Mathematics at 10+2 or in the degree.
